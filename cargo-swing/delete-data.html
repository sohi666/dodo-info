Delete my data

Cargo Swing · Consultation Behtoyi Inc. · Updated November 23, 2026

Do you have data with us?

Only if you tapped Allow on the "Help us improve Cargo Swing" question in the game. If you tapped No thanks, or never saw the question, we have nothing about you. Cargo Swing has no accounts.

How to have it deleted

  1. Stop sending: in the game, open Settings and turn Statistics off. Nothing more is sent from that moment.
  2. Ask us to delete what was sent: email support@cibinc.ca with the subject "Delete my Cargo Swing data". Tell us your device (for example iPhone 15 or Galaxy A07) and roughly when you played, so we can find the statistics tied to your device's random ID.
  3. We confirm by email and delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

All gameplay statistics linked to your random ID: levels and events played, results, coins earned and spent, screens opened, and the technical details sent with them (device model, system and game version, language, approximate country, performance data).

What is kept

Only totals that no longer relate to any player (for example "how many players finished level 40"). Nothing linked to you is kept after deletion; without a request, statistics are deleted after at most 24 months.

Progress on your device

Your levels, coins and settings are stored only on your phone. Deleting the app erases them; we never receive them.
