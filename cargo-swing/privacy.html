Privacy policy

Effective November 11, 2026 · Cargo Swing

What we collect

Nothing. Cargo Swing runs entirely on your device and sends no data: no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no network connection.

What stays on your device

Your progress (levels cleared, coins, events played) and your settings are saved locally. Deleting the app erases them.

Person in charge of personal information (Québec Law 25)

Sohrab Behtoyi, Consultation Behtoyi Inc. · support@cibinc.ca

Changes

If a future version collects any data, this page will be updated before that version ships.
