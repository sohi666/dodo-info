Privacy policy

Updated November 24, 2026 · Cargo Swing

In short

Cargo Swing has no account and works offline. Gameplay statistics are sent only if you tap Allow on the "Help us improve Cargo Swing" question. Some players also see a shop: optional purchases through the App Store or Google Play, and ads from Google. Those ads are never personalized, and the game never asks to track you across other apps or websites.

Statistics, only if you allow them

  • Gameplay statistics: levels and events started, won, failed, retried or left; stars; coins earned and spent on boosters; purchases (which product, and its price) and ads watched; help used on hard levels (the demo moves, the free dock spot); the crane operator you pick, ranks and trophies earned; whether the intro and port videos were watched or skipped; screens opened.
  • A random ID created by the analytics software (and, on iPhone, the app's vendor identifier), so we can count players and sessions without knowing who you are.
  • Technical details: device model, system version, game version, language, connection type, approximate country (derived from your IP address), basic performance and error data.

We use these statistics only to tune level difficulty, balance the game and fix bugs. You can turn them off anytime in Settings → Statistics.

Ads (players with the shop)

Players with the shop can watch a video ad, by choice, for an extra dock spot after a failed level, and from level 20 a short ad sometimes appears between levels (never right after a failed level). Any purchase turns these between-level ads off.

Ads come from Google AdMob (Google LLC, United States). We always ask Google for non-personalized ads, limited to family-friendly content. Even so, to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's software receives:

  • your IP address, from which an approximate location can be estimated;
  • the ads shown and your interactions with them (taps, video views);
  • app and device performance and crash data;
  • device identifiers limited to apps of the same developer (the app set ID on Android, the vendor identifier on iPhone). The game never gives Google your advertising ID: it does not request it on Android and never asks for tracking permission on iPhone.

Google handles this data under its own privacy policy, linked above. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form appears before any ad, and Settings → Ad privacy choices lets you change your answer.

Purchases (players with the shop)

Purchases are optional and processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own terms. We never see your name, payment details or store account. The game receives only which product you bought, a transaction number and the store's confirmation; it keeps the transaction numbers on your device so each purchase is credited once. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google.

Who processes it

Statistics are processed for us by GameAnalytics ApS (Denmark), a service provider acting on our instructions. Ads are served by Google LLC (United States). Purchases are processed by Apple Inc. or Google LLC (United States). These servers are outside Québec; before sending data outside Québec we assessed that it is adequately protected. We do not sell or rent your data.

How long we keep it

Statistics: no longer than needed to improve the game, and at most 24 months; after that they are deleted or kept only as totals that no longer relate to any player. Ad data is kept by Google as described in its policy. Store purchase records are kept by Apple or Google.

What stays on your device

Your progress (levels cleared, coins, stars, events played), your crew (operator, rank, trophies), your settings (language, sound and music volume), whether your game shows the shop, and your purchase records are saved locally. Deleting the app erases them; the Starter bundle comes back on the same store account (on iPhone, with Restore purchases in the shop).

Your choices and rights

Turn statistics off in Settings → Statistics; sending stops immediately. Video ads are always your choice, and any purchase turns the between-level ads off. To access, correct or delete statistics about you, write to support@cibinc.ca. Because they are linked only to a random ID, we may ask for your device and the approximate dates you played to find them. You may also contact the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec.

Changes

If a future version collects more, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the game will ask you again.
