Privacy policy

Updated November 23, 2026 · Cargo Swing

In short

Cargo Swing works fully offline and collects nothing unless you tap Allow on the "Help us improve Cargo Swing" question. No account, no ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking across other apps or websites. You can change your answer anytime in Settings → Statistics.

What we collect, only if you allow it

  • Gameplay statistics: levels and events started, won, failed, retried or left; stars; coins earned and spent on boosters; whether the intro was skipped; screens opened.
  • A random ID created by the analytics software (and, on iPhone, the app's vendor identifier), so we can count players and sessions without knowing who you are.
  • Technical details: device model, system version, game version, language, connection type, approximate country (derived from your IP address), basic performance and error data.

We never collect your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location or advertising identifier. We use these statistics only to tune level difficulty and fix bugs.

Who processes it

Statistics are processed for us by GameAnalytics ApS (Denmark), a service provider acting on our instructions, on servers outside Québec (in the European Union and other locations GameAnalytics uses). Before sending data outside Québec we assessed that it is adequately protected. We do not sell, rent or share it with anyone else.

How long we keep it

No longer than needed to improve the game, and at most 24 months; after that it is deleted or kept only as totals that no longer relate to any player.

What stays on your device

Your progress (levels cleared, coins, events played) and your settings are saved locally. Deleting the app erases them.

Your choices and rights

Turn statistics off in Settings → Statistics; sending stops immediately. To access, correct or delete statistics about you, write to support@cibinc.ca. Because they are linked only to a random ID, we may ask for your device and the approximate dates you played to find them. You may also contact the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec.

Person in charge of personal information (Québec Law 25)

Sohrab Behtoyi, Consultation Behtoyi Inc. · support@cibinc.ca

Changes

If a future version collects more (for example ads or purchases), this page will be updated before that version ships, and the game will ask you again.
