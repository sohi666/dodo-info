A harbour puzzle

Cargo Swing

Tap a container and the crane swings it onto the ship that wants its goods. No ship for it yet? It waits on the dock, which has only five spots. Plan ahead: the next ship always waits offshore.

  • 200 levels that keep getting harder, each checked to have a winning solution.
  • An event every five levels: Gold Rush, Pipeline or a Pirate Raid.
  • No ads, no purchases, no lives, no account. Plays offline, in English and French.
