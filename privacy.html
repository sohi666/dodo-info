Privacy policy

Effective November 9, 2026 · Dodo Planet, version 1.0

What we collect

Nothing. Dodo Planet runs entirely on your device and sends no data: no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no network connection.

What stays on your device

Your progress (solved planets, stars, settings, Daily Planet results) is saved locally. Deleting the app erases it.

Sharing

The “Copy result” button puts a short text on your clipboard, only when you tap it.

Person in charge of personal information (Québec Law 25)

Sohrab Behtoyi, Consultation Behtoyi Inc. · support@cibinc.ca

Changes

If a future version collects any data, this page will be updated before that version ships.
